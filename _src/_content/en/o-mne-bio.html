I am a board-certified plastic surgeon based in Bratislava. I cover the full spectrum of aesthetic and reconstructive surgery — from breast augmentation and liposuction, through blepharoplasty and otoplasty, to more complex procedures such as abdominoplasty with diastasis repair or gynecomastia.

My specializations also include hand surgery — above all carpal tunnel syndrome and Dupuytren’s contracture. For breast augmentation I use the modern Preservé™ method, which focuses on a gentle approach and a natural result. I also have a special interest in lipedema, a condition that requires a sensitive and expert approach.

I studied at the Faculty of Medicine of Comenius University in Bratislava (2012–2018). In 2023 I obtained a Master of Public Health (MPH) degree at the European Business & Management Institute in Prague, and in 2024 I passed the specialist examination in plastic surgery. During my studies I completed clinical placements in Germany.

As a doctor I keep learning — I regularly attend national and international congresses and professional training, including masterclass workshops in Marbella and Düsseldorf. I speak fluent English and German, which gives me access to the latest knowledge first-hand. Since February 2025 I have been part of the eveclinic team.
