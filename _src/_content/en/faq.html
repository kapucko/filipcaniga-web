During the consultation we discuss your wishes and expectations together, I carry out a physical examination and we propose an individual treatment plan. You will receive detailed information about the procedure, recovery and possible risks. The consultation is non-binding.
The date of surgery is agreed individually according to your preferences and current availability. It is usually a few weeks to a few months after the consultation.
Prices vary depending on the type and extent of the procedure. The exact price is set after a personal consultation, once your specific situation has been assessed. Prices include pre-operative examinations, the procedure itself, anaesthesia costs and post-operative check-ups.
Procedures are performed at an accredited clinic in Bratislava. The address and details will be provided once the date of surgery has been agreed. Parking is available near the clinic.

I use premium implants from established European manufacturers with CE certification and a long-term warranty. During the consultation we discuss the different implant types, sizes and shapes in detail and choose the most suitable option for you together.
The length of recovery depends on the type of procedure. Minor procedures (e.g. blepharoplasty) allow a return to work within 5–7 days, while larger operations (facelift, abdominoplasty) require 2–4 weeks of rest. You will receive detailed instructions before surgery.
Aesthetic procedures are generally not covered by health insurance. Exceptions may include reconstructive surgery after cancer treatment or certain functional operations (e.g. septorhinoplasty for impaired nasal breathing). I recommend checking with your insurer individually.
The minimum age depends on the specific procedure and on the physical and psychological maturity of the patient. Most aesthetic operations are recommended from the age of 18; for some procedures (otoplasty) younger patients can be operated on with the written consent of a legal guardian.
You will receive detailed written instructions before the operation. In general: you need to complete pre-operative examinations, stop smoking at least 4 weeks before surgery, stop taking blood-thinning medication (aspirin, ibuprofen) and come with an empty stomach on the day of the operation.
