1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone or tablet) when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences for a certain period of time.

2. Which cookies we use

2.1 Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function properly. Without them, some parts of the website would not work correctly. Their use does not require your consent.

  • fc_cookies_accepted — stores your cookie consent choice (1 year)

2.2 Analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website. All information is anonymised. We only use them with your consent.

  • Google Analytics — measuring traffic and behaviour on the website

2.3 Marketing cookies

These cookies are used to display relevant advertising. We only use them with your consent.

3. Managing cookies

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See which cookies are stored
  • Delete specific or all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block third-party cookies

Please note that blocking some cookies may affect how the website works.

4. Third-party cookies

Our website may also use third-party cookies (e.g. Google Analytics). These cookies are governed by the privacy policies of the respective third parties.

5. Changes to this cookie policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will inform you of any changes through a notice on the website. We recommend checking this page regularly.

6. Contact
